The final Ultimate Excel Personal Budget has the following main features:

**Budget Planning:**

  1. Zero-based budget planning
  2. Fully customizable income, expenses, and savings categories
  3. Multi-period approach to set the right expectations for every single month

**Budget Tracking:**

  1. Smart table to assign all your money flows to your defined categories
  2. Option to “shift” late monthly income to the next month
     
**Budget Dashboard:**

  1. Dynamic aggregation of all planned and tracked data
  2. Smart budget breakdown and chart visualizations
  3. Fully interactive for selecting different period and visualization views
